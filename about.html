About Flappy Sky

Flappy Sky is a one-tap arcade game where you guide a rocket through obstacles, collect coins, and try to beat your best score.

The game includes themed maps and unlockable rocket and pipe designs. Some visual designs are drawn by the game code rather than loaded as separate image files.

Flappy Sky is an independent game project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the creators of other arcade games.
