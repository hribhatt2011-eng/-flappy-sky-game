How to Play

  1. Open Flappy Sky and tap Play Now.
  2. Tap the screen to give your rocket an upward boost.
  3. Guide the rocket through the gaps between pipes. Avoid touching obstacles.
  4. Collect coins during a run and use available coins to unlock eligible designs.
  5. Use the pause button when you need a break. After a run, choose Play Again or return to the main menu.

On a computer, use the Space key if keyboard controls are available. On a phone or tablet, tap the game area.

Tip: use steady, well-timed taps and watch the next gap.
