Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

This policy describes the current Flappy Sky game website. The game may store gameplay information such as your best score, coin balance, and settings in your browser's local storage so that they can remain available on that device.

Information and accounts

The current game does not ask players to create an account or enter their name, email address, or payment-card details in the game.

Advertising and payments

Advertising and real-money payment processing are not currently connected in this game build. The Premium screen is a demonstration only; it does not take a payment or grant a paid purchase. If advertising, analytics, or payment services are added later, this policy will be updated to explain the relevant providers and data practices before those features are enabled.

Third-party hosting

The website is hosted using GitHub Pages. The hosting provider may process technical information as described in its own privacy documentation. Please review GitHub's privacy information for details.

Children and parents

Parents or guardians should supervise children's use of online services. Do not submit personal or payment information through unofficial messages or links claiming to represent Flappy Sky.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the website's features or data practices change. The updated date will be shown at the top of this page.
